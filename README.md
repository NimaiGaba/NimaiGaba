# 🚀 Selected Projects

### 🏗️ Enterprise Analytics Engineering Pipeline (Jaffle Shop)

End-to-end **dbt pipeline** transforming raw QSR franchise data (6 source tables) into a **Kimball star schema** across bronze/silver/gold layers, with item-level gross-profit facts joining prices to ingredient supply costs.

**Tech:** `dbt` `SQL` `MetricFlow` `SQLFluff` `Dimensional Modeling`

- MetricFlow time spine, cohort-retention and 80/20 store-revenue Pareto analyses
- Data quality enforced with dbt tests and SQLFluff linting

🔗 [View Code](https://github.com/NimaiGaba/REPO_NAME)

### 📉 SaaS Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) & Churn Risk Engine

Python EDA pipeline, SQL LTV cohorts and a **Logistic Regression** risk-scoring model feeding a **Power BI** dashboard.

**Tech:** `Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `SQLAlchemy` `Scikit-Learn` `Power BI`

- Month-to-month accounts without add-ons churned at **41.8%** vs **2.7%** for annual plans
- More than 3 early support tickets with >24h resolution meant **3× churn risk**
- Recommended a 20% annual-upgrade offer and weekend onboarding coverage

🔗 [View Code](https://github.com/NimaiGaba/REPO_NAME)

### 🍔 QSR Sales & Inventory Optimization

Analyzed **48,000+ orders ($817,860 GMV over 12 months)** in SQL and built interactive Power BI dashboards to track sales KPIs and validate data integrity.

**Tech:** `SQL` `Power BI` `DAX` `KPI Analysis`

- Lunch and dinner rushes drive **62%** of daily revenue; the top 3 of 32 items drive **24%** of GMV
- AOV rises from $16.75 to $19.20 on weekends
- Recommended pre-prepping top sellers and weekday up-sells

🔗 [View Code](https://github.com/NimaiGaba/REPO_NAME)
